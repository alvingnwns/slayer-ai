Controls

Hand gestures

  • Close your fist — draw the katana
  • Hold still — charge the Breathing Style
  • Swing fast — unleash the attack
  • Open your hand — sheathe (Hold mode) · or hold an open palm ~2 s (Latch mode)

Mouse & keyboard

  • Move mouse — move the sword; drag or F — grip / draw
  • 1–4 / Tab — Breathing Style
  • Space hold to charge, release to attack · right-click attack
  • X sheathe · Q/E rotate · C calibrate angle
  • B camera background · M mute · D debug · S settings
Unofficial fan-made demo

Nichirin AR

Hold a Demon Slayer-inspired katana with your own hand and awaken four Breathing Styles — Flame, Water, Insect and Thunder.

About the camera

Hand tracking runs entirely on your device in this browser tab. Camera frames are never recorded, stored or uploaded. Your browser will ask for permission; you can switch the camera off at any time.

Tip: face a light source, keep your hand inside the frame and about an arm's length from the camera.
